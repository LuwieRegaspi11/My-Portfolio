01 · About

From troubleshooting hardware to shipping interfaces.

My path into web development didn't start with a browser — it started with a screwdriver. Before I wrote HTML, I was already comfortable opening up a machine, diagnosing what was wrong, and putting it back together properly. That habit of methodical troubleshooting is still how I approach a bug today.

I'm currently finishing my BSIT degree while building out my front-end and programming fundamentals through self-directed coursework. I care about writing code that's readable and interfaces that behave the way people expect — nothing flashy for its own sake.

I'm actively looking for an internship or entry-level role where I can put these fundamentals to work on a real team.
